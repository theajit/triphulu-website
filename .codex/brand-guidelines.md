# TripHulu brand guidelines

## Brand idea

TripHulu connects the optimism of travel with the clarity of an operating platform. The visual identity should feel modern, friendly, adventurous and trustworthy without making the B2B product resemble a consumer travel blog.

## Logo

The supplied primary logo combines the TripHulu wordmark, travel-smile icon and the line “Dream · Explore · Discover.”

- Primary asset: `/public/brand/triphulu-logo.png`
- Reference brand board: `/public/brand/triphulu-brand-board.png`
- Use the logo on white, very light neutral or deep navy backgrounds.
- Preserve the original proportions. Never stretch, redraw, recolor, rotate or add effects inside the mark.
- Maintain clear space around the logo of at least the height of one wordmark letter.
- Use the full lockup at 150 px wide or larger. At smaller sizes, a separately approved icon-only asset should be used.
- Do not crop the tagline from this bitmap to manufacture an alternate lockup.

## Color palette

| Role | Color | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Indigo | `#6366F1` |
| Secondary | Violet | `#A855F7` |
| Accent | Pink | `#EC4899` |
| Primary text | Midnight | `#191B2E` |
| Dark surface | Deep navy | `#0E1021` |
| Light surface | Cloud | `#F7F7FC` |
| Muted text | Slate | `#667085` |

The three brand colors may be used as a left-to-right gradient: `#6366F1 → #A855F7 → #EC4899`. Use the gradient selectively for primary actions, signature accents and branded moments—not as a background for every section.

## Typography

- Headings: Manrope, semibold or bold.
- Body and interface copy: DM Sans, regular through semibold.
- Keep headings direct and operational. Avoid decorative display styles that reduce credibility for agency owners.

## Voice

- Clear, practical and optimistic.
- Lead with business outcomes and connected workflows.
- Use travel language as a human accent, not as consumer-holiday advertising.
- Avoid unsupported AI, payment-guarantee or universal integration claims.

## Imagery and interface

- Prefer authentic operational interface views, route motifs and structured journey diagrams.
- Never use customer PII, tenant data, credentials or private QA evidence.
- Consumer-style travel imagery should support a specific story and remain secondary to the operator workflow.
- Use rounded forms sparingly; the smile-and-aircraft icon supplies the friendly character while layouts stay disciplined.
