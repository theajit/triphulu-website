# Product decisions

- TripHulu positioning: “Sales, booking, payment and operations platform for tour operators.”
- The marketing site is static and separate from the authenticated product.
- Customer and tenant data is never used in public marketing screenshots.
- Feature claims must be repository-verified and approved before launch.
- Payment providers and communication delivery depend on tenant configuration and must not be presented as universal.
- Automated regression complements, and does not replace, manual release assurance.
- The approved visual palette is indigo `#6366F1`, violet `#A855F7` and pink `#EC4899`, supported by midnight and cloud neutrals.
- The supplied TripHulu logo is the source asset; it must not be redrawn or distorted.
